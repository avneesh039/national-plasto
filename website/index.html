Step-by-Step Production Process

FROM MOULD TO MARKET

A disciplined 7-stage industrial quality sequence from raw polymer resin to final customer dispatch.

STAGE 01

Raw Material

100% prime virgin polypropylene granules with UV masterbatch compounding.

STAGE 02

Tooling Setup

Hydraulic clamping and core alignment calibrated to micron tolerances.

STAGE 03

Moulding

High-pressure melt injection with proportional valve control to eliminate voids.

STAGE 04

Inspection

Optical dimensional auditing and surface sink-mark checks on cooling.

STAGE 05

Finishing

Precision gate cutting and smooth edge polishing for ergonomic comfort.

STAGE 06

Packaging

Heavy-gauge protective poly-wrap and nested stacking preventing transit scratches.

STAGE 07

Dispatch

Palletized warehouse loading and scheduled logistics across pan-India corridors.
