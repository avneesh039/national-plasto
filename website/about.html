BUILT ON EXPERIENCE. ENGINEERED FOR INDIA.

National Plasto is an Indian polymer engineering powerhouse, designing and moulding high-durability polymer furniture, storage and utility articles, and custom precision components.

From precision tool & die making to high-tonnage micro-robotic injection presses, we operate an integrated 100,000+ sq. ft. manufacturing complex in Kolkata.

Every product is molded from 100% prime virgin polymers with UV inhibitors and impact modifiers—engineered to withstand extreme Indian temperatures, heavy institutional loads, and daily multi-generational use.
